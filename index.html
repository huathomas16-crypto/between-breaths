Between Breaths

Design
Context

For most people, “yielding” means making compromises and giving space to others. Yet we rarely think about yielding to ourselves.

Personal space is essential, but we constantly fill it with unnecessary objects, gradually losing the emptiness around us.

Through rhythmic, breathing-like movements, this project gently prevents clutter from occupying its surrounding space. Rather than simply organizing objects, it encourages people to reclaim space for themselves while becoming a kinetic sculpture that blends into their living environment.

Care Brief

For me, care is about paying attention to yourself and recognizing when you need a moment to slow down. It means giving yourself space instead of always trying to keep up or push forward. Sometimes, caring for yourself is simply allowing yourself to pause. At the same time, care can take many forms depending on different people, situations, and needs. Overall, I see care as being aware, responsive, and willing to make small changes for yourself and others.

Design
Concept

Breathing is one of the most fundamental forms of self-care. Just as the rib cage expands to create space for the lungs, Between Breaths expands and contracts to preserve personal space. Its gentle, rhythmic movement transforms a mechanical function into a living, sculptural expression of care.

Inspiration

Form
Exploration

I explored various mechanisms for creating and preserving space. Balloon-like structures offered simple expansion but were too fragile, while scissor-ring mechanisms were effective but overly complex. Ultimately, I chose an umbrella-inspired structure for its strength, stability, and mechanical feasibility. I also incorporated bending elements to create softer, more elegant curves, giving the product a more sculptural appearance.

Design
Development

Although the fabrication process may seem straightforward, many critical adjustments were made along the way. Enlarging the base for better stability, replacing adhesive connections with screws, and refining the structure of each fin were all essential to the final outcome. These seemingly small decisions played a crucial role in the functionality and reliability of Between Breaths.

Outcomes &
Reflection

Between Breaths was an important exploration of mechanical structures, and I am proud that it achieved both its intended function and the sculptural appearance I envisioned.

This project also changed how I think about care. I realized that care does not always require adding something to our lives; sometimes, it means protecting the empty space we already have.

The fabrication process taught me the importance of early material testing, as unexpected issues with adhesives and structural connections created time pressure. In future projects, I hope to test materials earlier and incorporate sensors and microcontrollers to make my designs more interactive.
